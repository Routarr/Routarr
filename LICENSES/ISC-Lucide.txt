Routarr's logo is Lucide's git-branch icon (https://lucide.dev/icons/git-branch),
drawn in these files and in the images rendered from them:

- .github/assets/logo.svg, the README's
- frontend/public/favicon.svg and frontend/src/components/Sidebar.svelte, the
  interface's
- site/public/assets/favicon.svg, from which site/icons.mjs renders
  site/public/favicon.ico, site/public/assets/favicon-32.png and
  site/public/assets/apple-touch-icon.png
- site/src/components/icons/Brand.astro, the showcase site's header
- site/og.html, from which site/og.mjs renders the link preview cards,
  site/public/assets/og-<language>.png
